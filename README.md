## Slide 1 — Title

### NextGen Graphic Designing

**Creative design that moves your brand forward.**

NextGen Graphic Designing is a Kenya-based creative design service helping small businesses, startups, entrepreneurs, and individuals create professional visual identities and digital marketing materials.

**Founder:** Martin Mulekya
**Location:** Kenya


## Slide 2 — The Problem

### Small businesses struggle to look professional online.

Many small businesses and startups need quality graphic design but face challenges such as:

* Professional design services can be expensive.
* Business owners may not have design skills or dedicated designers.
* Inconsistent logos, colors, posters, and social-media graphics can weaken a brand's identity.
* Creating marketing materials repeatedly can take valuable time.
* Some businesses rely on generic templates that make their branding look similar to competitors.

**The result:** Businesses may have good products or services but struggle to communicate their value visually.


## Slide 3 — The Solution

### Professional design made accessible to growing businesses.

**NextGen Graphic Designing** provides practical, affordable design services tailored to small businesses and startups.

Services can include:

* Logo and brand identity design
* Social-media graphics
* Posters and promotional materials
* Business cards and digital marketing materials
* Website graphics
* Custom visual content
* Ongoing design support for businesses

### Our approach

**Understand the business → Create the design → Get feedback → Refine → Deliver**

The goal is to help businesses build a consistent and recognizable visual identity without needing an in-house designer.

## Slide 6 — Product / Service

### What does NextGen actually provide?

Customers can request designs through the NextGen platform.

### Example customer journey

**1. Choose a service**
Customer identifies what they need: logo, poster, social-media design, branding, etc.

**2. Submit requirements**
Customer provides business information, preferred style, content, images, and other requirements.

**3. Design development**
NextGen creates the initial design.

**4. Review and feedback**
The customer reviews the design and provides feedback.

**5. Final delivery**
The approved design is delivered in appropriate digital formats.

### Website

The NextGen website acts as the company's online portfolio and customer contact point, allowing potential customers to explore services and previous work.


## Slide 7 — Business Model

### How will NextGen make money?

NextGen will primarily operate using a **service-based revenue model**.

### Revenue streams

**Individual design services**

* Customers pay for specific design projects.

**Design packages**

* Bundled services such as logo + business card + social-media graphics.

**Branding packages**

* More comprehensive packages for businesses establishing their visual identity.

**Recurring design support**

* Businesses can pay for ongoing monthly design services.

### Example model

**Basic → Standard → Premium**

This gives customers different price and service levels while creating opportunities for larger projects and recurring revenue.

*Final pricing will be determined through customer research and testing.*


## Slide 8 — Go-To-Market Strategy

### How will we acquire our first customers?

The initial strategy is focused on direct and low-cost customer acquisition.

### 1. Personal network

Reach out to entrepreneurs, business owners, friends, colleagues, and professional contacts.

### 2. Direct outreach

Contact small businesses that could benefit from improved branding or marketing materials.

### 3. Social-media communities

Participate in relevant Kenyan business and entrepreneurship communities.
